# Shooting Diary – Quick start

*Updated 21 Oct 2026 · for people using the app; setup and admin are in the [README](../README.md).*

## Getting started

1. Open **diary.osseyman.com**, tap *Ask for one* and pick a username and password (10+ characters). Log in once the admin approves. If the admin made your account, leave the password empty the first time and choose your own.
2. Install it: *Install app* on the home screen (Android Chrome), or Share → *Add to Home Screen* (iPhone). Long-press the icon for shortcuts.
3. The home screen has a tile per part with a short summary. The bar at the top switches between Diary, Velocity and Zeroes.

Good to know:

- It works offline at the range; changes are sent when you're back online.
- What you type is kept until you press Save. Closed a form by accident? Reopen it and your changes come back (*Discard* drops them).
- The phone's Back button closes the open form. Long forms have Save at the top and bottom.

## Diary

1. Add your guns once: *Guns* → *+ Add gun* with name, type, calibre and firing mode. *Shots before the diary* starts the round count where the gun already is.
2. **+ New visit**: today's date and time are filled in. Pick the place and organiser from the recent chips or type them.
3. Tap a gun to add its row and set the shots (the field, or − / + in steps of 5). *+ Other row* is for anything else.
4. **Save**. Tap a visit later to edit or delete it.

Good to know:

- Only the date is required.
- **Signature:** someone, e.g. the range officer, can sign the visit at the bottom with their name and a finger signature. The corner button opens a big sideways pad. Signed visits are marked *SIGNED*.
- **Export** makes a PDF or CSV in Finnish (the official record for a licence application) or English: last 2 years, a year, custom dates or everything. Signatures are printed with the time they were made.
- **My details** holds your name and licence numbers for the export.

## Velocity

1. **Import:** in ShotView, session → Share → *Shooting Diary* (Android, app installed with Chrome). Or tap **+ Import velocities** and pick files or a folder: ShotView .xls/.xlsx, velocity workbooks, Kestrel CSV weather.
2. Check the gun and load for each string in the preview (*+ New load…* creates one), then *Save selected*. Importing the same file twice is safe.
3. **Browse:** gun buttons filter the page. Pick a load (listed by calibre) for its average with 95 % interval, SD per day and velocity vs temperature in m/s per °C. *Charge ladders* buttons show velocity by charge.

Good to know:

- A handload is bullet, brass, powder, charge, primer and COAL; its name is built for you. Saving a name that already exists offers a merge.
- Tap a string, then a shot, to leave out a flier; the numbers update at once.
- Weather can be added by hand if the import had none.

## Zeroes

1. **Set up:** *+ Scope* (unit, click value, zero stop, turret turn size), then *+ Add position* for each rifle and load with its dial readings.
2. **Swap:** pick *Scope is on* and *Move to*. It shows the clicks to dial and where the caps end up. Dial, mount, tap *Mounted on …*, then shoot a check group: *On zero ✓* or *Off – adjust…*.
3. **Correct a zero:** open the position → *Adjust zero* → tap the correction you dialed → *Save adjustment*. *Undo* reverts it.

Good to know:

- Shot this load with the turrets at 0 / 0? Choose *From 0 / 0* and tap the correction counted from there; the app works out the move.
- Tap the correction you dialed, not where the group hit: a group high needs DOWN.
- No signal? Changes are kept on the phone and sent later.
- *PDF card* prints all positions and swaps for the range bag; *Notes* holds anything else.

## Guns loaned

1. **+ Loan**: date and match or event.
2. One line per friend: their name, your gun (calibre and ammo price fill in) or *Not my gun – ammo only* with the calibre, and the shots.
3. **Save**. Tick *Paid* when they pay; *Share cost* sends them the breakdown.

Their shots add to your gun's round count, not to your own shooting record. *Ammo prices* sets the price per round for each calibre.

## Click calculator

1. Enter the distance (m, yd or ft).
2. Enter how far the group was from the aiming point and tap *High/Low* and *Left/Right* (mm, cm or inches).
3. Pick the click value; your own scopes are listed first.

The result reads like **UP 3 / RIGHT 1**, with exact clicks, MRAD and MOA below. *Reset* clears the offsets.

## Your account

- *Password* and *My data* (download everything as one file) are on the home screen; the door icon at the top logs out.
- Forgot your password? Ask the admin to reset it, then choose a new one at the next login.
- Only you see your data. *Privacy* on the login page says what is stored.
