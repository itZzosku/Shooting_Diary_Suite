Privacy

Shooting Diary is a small, private tool run by one person for a few shooting friends. This page says what it stores about you and who can see it.

What is stored

  • Your account: username, your password as a one-way hash (it can't be read back), when the account was made and when you last logged in.
  • What you enter: your name and licence numbers (SRA, IPSC, hunter number) if you fill them in, your guns, range visits, velocity strings and loads, weather you add to them, scope zeroes and notes, and guns loaned with your friends' names and what they owe.
  • Henkilötunnus: you can type it into Export to print it on that one PDF or CSV. It is never stored – not on the server and not in your browser – and the field is emptied when Export closes.
  • Signatures: if someone signs one of your visits (optional), their name and the signature they draw are stored with that visit and printed in your PDF export.
  • Cookies: one login cookie (keeps you logged in) and one that holds your username so the app keeps each account's offline data apart on a shared phone. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • On your phone: changes made without a connection wait in the browser's storage until they are sent, plus a few settings such as the last filter you used.
  • Server logs: the web server may keep ordinary access logs (IP address, time, page) for running and securing the site.

Who can see it

  • Only you see your data in the app. Every account has its own separate database; other users can't see or change it.
  • The site's admin runs the server and can technically read the data files and backups. They are not looked at or used for anything else, except to fix a problem or when you ask.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or handed over to anyone, unless the law requires it.
  • Everything the site uses (fonts, the PDF tool) comes from this server – your browser doesn't contact any other company while you use it.

Where and how long

  • The data is on the admin's own server. It is backed up weekly; the server keeps the last 12 weekly copies (about three months), and a copy of each weekly backup is also emailed to the admin's own mailbox.
  • It is kept while you have an account. When an account is deleted, its data is removed from the app at once; a final copy and its backups on the server are removed after 30 days. Copies in older backups disappear as the backups roll over.

Your choices

  • Download everything any time: landing page → My data (one JSON file).
  • Correct or delete entries yourself in the app; leave the licence fields empty if you don't want them stored.
  • Delete your account and its data: ask the admin.
